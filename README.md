# 4MATR Academy

> The front door to the 4MATR ecosystem.

4MATR is an open ecosystem at the intersection of **finance, AI, and Education**.

This repository contains the landing page that brings the different parts of 4MATR Academy together in one place.

## What is 4MATR?

4MATR exists to make finance more accessible, practical and intelligent.

We learn. We build. We experiment. We share.

The ecosystem brings together education, financial modelling, AI experimentation, resources, community and content.

---

## 4MATR Ecosystem

### Learn

**4MATR Academy**  
Practical learning across finance, accounting, valuation, modelling and AI.

**The 4MATR Brief**  
A newsletter exploring finance, companies, models, AI and practical investing concepts.

**AI Financial Engineering Handbook**  
An open-source resource documenting how we approach financial modelling and engineering with AI.

### Build

**AI Finance Lab**  
Our experimentation space for AI, financial modelling, data, APIs and software engineering.

### Community

**4MATR Learning Community**  
An open community for people learning and exploring finance together.

**4MATR Guild**  
A private community for deeper discussion, collaboration and experimentation.

### Content

4MATR also publishes across:

- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Facebook
- Instagram
- GitHub
